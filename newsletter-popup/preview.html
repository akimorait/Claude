How the A/B test works

  1. Each new visitor gets one design. It's picked at random using the shares you set, and they keep seeing the same one on every visit.

  2. Every view, close and sign-up is counted. They go to your Google Analytics with the design letter in the name, like popup_view_b and popup_signup_b. Nothing to set up.

  3. Every subscriber is tagged in Shopify. Look under Customers for popup-A to popup-E. They also keep your customer-code tag.

  4. Pick the winner. After a few weeks, set the losing designs to 0% in the theme editor. No code changes needed.
